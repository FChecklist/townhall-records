You are offline

Your phone or laptop cannot reach the internet right now. This is usually a Wi-Fi or mobile data problem, not a problem with Townhall.

Still works
  • Pages you already opened on this device.
  • What you typed on a screen stays there until you close it.
  • The notice and result pages you opened before.
Does not work until you are back online
  • Voting.
  • Signing in, and the 6-digit e-mail code.
  • Checking people in.
  • Sending any form.

What to do

  1. Check Wi-Fi or switch on mobile data.
  2. Do not close this tab if you were in the middle of something.
  3. Press the button below. If it still fails, walk to where the signal is better and try again.
  4. Voting day: tell the Attendance Monitor. A paper ballot is the back-up.
