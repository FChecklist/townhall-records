Why a record

Most quarrels are not about the decision. They are about what happened.

Who was present. Who voted. Which rule applied. What the count was. Townhall Records answers these questions before anyone has to ask them.

Accountability

Every action has a role, a date and a time. Nothing happens quietly, and nothing can be quietly changed afterwards.

Transparency

The full record is published when the event ends. Any member can download it as a PDF and read every step for themselves.

Rules

Your bye-laws are set before the first vote. Each step is checked against them, and the report shows the rules that were used.

Paper or phone

With paper ballots. Without paper ballots. Your choice.

Some members love paper. Some will only use a phone. Townhall Records treats both with the same care, and puts both on the same record.

With paper ballots

Members vote on paper, as they always have. Your officers enter each ballot into the record, and the count includes them. The paper stays with you as a second proof.

Without paper ballots

Members vote on their own phones. Nothing to print, nothing to carry, nothing to count by hand. You can also use both in the same election.

A record you can stand behind

Dated. Verified. Impossible to quietly rewrite.

  • Every entry carries the date and time it happened.
  • Verification details sit beside the entry: who checked, how, and when.
  • Once published, the record cannot be edited by anyone, including us.
  • Anyone can download the full record and check it for themselves.

Voting is secret

We record that a flat has voted, never what it voted. The ballot box holds no name and no time. Each voter gets a receipt code that proves the ballot was counted without showing the choice.

A record like this is made to be examined, by members, by auditors and, if it ever comes to it, by a court or an authority. It is very hard to wave away. How much weight a record carries in a particular dispute depends on your bye-laws and the law where you live, so keep your advocate in the loop. We give you the evidence, in order.
