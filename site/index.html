Software for associations and societies

Order in every meeting.

Townhall is simple software for your society's meeting or election. Residents vote on their phone or on paper. Every step is recorded, and anyone can download the complete record.

Six steps, the same as the real room

1

Rules

Your bye-laws set the rules before anyone votes.

2

Attendance

Each person is checked in at the door.

3

Quorum

Who is present is counted and shown live.

4

Vote

On the phone, on paper, or both.

5

Count

The software counts. Nobody counts by hand.

6

Publish

The complete record is published as soon as the event ends.

Who it is for

Apartment owners' associations, resident welfare associations, housing societies and co-operative societies. Annual meetings, special meetings, committee meetings and elections.

What you can count on

  • Residents need no account: one link, one code.
  • No app to install. It works on any phone.
  • Names and numbers are hidden as **** on every public page.
  • The complete record, with every detail, can be downloaded by anyone and saved as a PDF.
  • Private details are encrypted and erased on request or after one year.
