# Brand and positioning (working name: Townhall)

**Owner's brief (2026-10-23):** Townhall. Rules - Attendance - Quorum - Vote - Count - Publish. Offline and online both available. Make life easier for everyone. A meeting and election management software service for AOAs, RWAs, HOAs and societies, designed to mimic the real world. The brand should bring out that this is a *service that prevents fights*.

## Name
* **Working name: Townhall**, kept in one constant (`packages/engine/src/brand.ts`) so a rename is a one-line change; the page, PDF, e-mails and terms use it.
* **Cleared? No.** Not checked: trademark classes 9, 42, 45 (India, US, UK, EU), domains, app stores, social handles. Concerns raised: "town hall" is a generic phrase (hard to own); to my knowledge Townhall.com is an established US political news site (verify); "townhome" communities could cause confusion. Fallbacks that keep the same message: Accord, FairHall, OpenGavel. Do a proper clearance before printing anything.

## The six steps (the product, the page tracker, the website strip)
**Rules, Attendance, Quorum, Vote, Count, Publish.** It is the real room's order. The public page shows these six as a picture tracker with the current one highlighted (`stage` in the model); the console's 8 setup steps lead up to the first of them.

## One-line promises (claims we can prove)
| Owner's wording | Use this on the website and in the app | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 100% data security | "Private details are encrypted. Every action is recorded. Nothing can be edited after the report is published." | "100% secure" is an absolute no honest vendor can prove and a regulator or a court can hold against us |
| 100% attendance validation | "Every person is checked in by an officer, by a two-screen code exchange that proves they are really there. The report shows how each flat was checked." | true and checkable on the report |
| 100% rule | "Your rules, applied exactly. Every report shows the rules used and checks the result against them." | we apply what the society entered; we do not decide what the law is |
| prevents fights | "Ends the argument about what happened: who was there, who voted, what the rules were, what the count was. All on record, provable by anyone." | disagreement about decisions will remain; disputes about facts are what we remove |
| mimic the real world | "Same order as the real room: rules, register, quorum, ballot, count, minutes. Online, on paper, or both." | true (see OFFLINE-AND-HANDSHAKE.md) |
| easier for everyone | "Eight simple steps for the Organizer; one screen at a time for officers and voters; 16 languages." | true for the design; the screens are still to be built |

## Hero copy (draft)
> **Townhall**
> Rules · Attendance · Quorum · Vote · Count · Publish
> **Order in every meeting. Proof in every vote.**
> Run your society's meeting or election online, on paper, or both. The report is published within two minutes, signed, and anyone can check it.
> For AOAs, RWAs, HOAs and housing societies.

## Words to avoid in public copy
"100% secure", "unhackable", "cheat-proof", "legally binding" (we record and check; legal effect depends on the society's bye-laws and the law), "guaranteed lawful". Use: tamper-evident, verifiable, transparent, on record.
