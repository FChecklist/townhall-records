## The common regime (blunt)
For NO State could "which regime do most flat-owner bodies use" be established from a source. What can be said from the texts read:
- Delhi: three regimes coexist (Delhi Apartment Ownership Act model bye-laws, co-operative group-housing societies, RWAs under the Societies Registration Act). The first two were read; the RWA regime was not.
- Tamil Nadu: the new 2022 Act + 2024 Rules (model bye-laws read in full) are the law for new projects; older bodies are co-operative or registered societies.
- Rajasthan, Odisha, Tamil Nadu: a new apartment statute (2015/2019, 2023, 2022) replaced an older one very recently.
- Everywhere else the apartment statutes only say "the bye-laws shall state the quorum", so the real numbers live in each building's own registered bye-laws.
Treat every "most common" statement as unverified.

## Weak points to know
- Many texts were OCR scans or secondary statute sites (lawgist, Indian Kanoon, legitquest, aggregators); a lawyer must compare with the gazette.
- Co-operative Acts consistently lock "one member one vote" and "no proxy" (locked in the engine blocks of Delhi, UP, Uttarakhand, Odisha, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, J&K, Sikkim, Tripura, Rajasthan, MP, Punjab co-operative packs). Apartment-ownership model bye-laws vary: Delhi, Maharashtra = in person only (not locked, because a departure with Administrator approval is possible); Tamil Nadu, Rajasthan, MP = proxy allowed.
- Quorum fractions were loaded as 3333 / 6666 basis points so exactly one-third / two-thirds satisfies them.
- Engine confidence is `low` or `medium` throughout; none is `high`.

## Engine gaps (law features the product cannot express)
- Different quorum or majority per business type (Tamil Nadu 1/3 vs 2/3 SGM; Rajasthan 2/3, 3/4 for elections, 90% reserved matters; Delhi/Maharashtra by-law 56 at 75% or 51% of the VALUE of units).
- Quorum with a head-count floor (Delhi 10 members, Tamil Nadu 3, Punjab 5, Telangana "not below one-tenth") rather than only a cap.
- Three resolution grades (ordinary, special, extra-ordinary of ALL members) and special-majority fractions as parameters.
- Quorum counted by members while voting is by Declaration percentage.
- Adjournment to a fixed same-day or next-day slot; a requisitioned meeting dissolved (Delhi, Meghalaya) instead of adjourned; a reduced quorum as a fraction of the original (UP 50%).
- Poll or secret ballot only on demand (Delhi one-third present; West Bengal 20%); show of hands for resolutions but secret ballot for elections; tie rules that differ for elections (lots / coin) and resolutions (casting vote).
- Arrears or attendance flags that remove the vote and candidacy (60 days Delhi / Maharashtra / Rajasthan / Punjab; six months UP).
- Elections run by an outside authority (Delhi returning officer appointed by the Registrar, State Co-operative Election Authority / Commission in Telangana, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, UP, Uttarakhand, Tripura, Sikkim).
- Reserved seats by class (SC/ST, OBC, women) as a single total only; staggered Board terms (one-third retire yearly; 3/2/1 years); terms stated as a ceiling ("not exceeding five years").
- Copies of notices to a Registrar / Competent Authority, and Competent Authority approval of bye-law changes.
- Minutes deadlines in working days; proxy deposit deadline; meeting-frequency duties (Board every month / quarter).
