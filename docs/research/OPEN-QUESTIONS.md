# OPEN-QUESTIONS

Research only, NOT legal advice. Ranked by importance (1 = decide first). "Who" says whether a lawyer or the owner must decide. Each has a recommendation.

| # | Question | Who | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"100% secret ballot" vs the host seeing "voter name, flat, voted YES/NO".** They contradict each other. Secrecy is required for elections in Maharashtra (Election Rules r.33), California (Civ. 5100), and FL/TX e-voting rules separate identity from secret ballots. | Owner (then lawyer) | Host sees *who voted* only; choices appear only as aggregate tallies. Keep a separate, labelled "open vote" mode for ordinary resolutions only where the society's bye-laws allow a show of hands. |
| 2 | **Is remote online voting lawful in India** (UP AOA/RWA, Maharashtra)? No primary text found permitting or banning it. Maharashtra's 2026 Rule 106C-13(3)(b) permits video attendance at general bodies, not online ballots; Election Rules 2014 mention secret ballot or EVM at polling. | Indian lawyer | Get a written opinion before selling in India. Meanwhile market India as "run and record" the meeting, and gate remote voting behind a society resolution adopting it plus a bye-law check. |
| 3 | **Maharashtra 2026 Rules were read only via a secondary reproduction**; new model bye-laws may replace bye-law numbers; the "51% of total members present" wording is ambiguous; sub-rule (f) duplicated. | Lawyer | Obtain the gazette PDF and the new model bye-laws; treat Maharashtra pack values as medium confidence until then. |
| 4 | **"Never delete anything"** conflicts with DPDP s.6(4), s.8(7), s.12(3) from 13 May 2027. | Owner (marketing) + lawyer | Promise permanence only for the result and the tamper-evident proof; personal data kept per a stated retention table. See `DPDP-AND-RECORDS.md` section 8. |
| 5 | **Proxy voters.** Maharashtra bars proxies (bye-law 105; Election Rule 33). UP is ambiguous (bye-law 10). US: CA only if bylaws allow and never in place of a ballot; FL allowed (dated, 90-day expiry); TX must be offered. | Owner + lawyer | Make proxy a per-pack and per-society switch; default OFF for `in-mh-coop-society`; require a signed/dated proxy form with named holder and meeting; record both identities. |
| 6 | **Extending the voting window by 30 minutes after notice.** Notices state deadlines (CA 30-day ballot notice, TX 20 days). An extension may breach the noticed time. | Lawyer | Allow extensions only if the notice reserved that right, log reason and time, and notify all voters. Cap the number of extensions. |
| 7 | **Independence of the election officer.** CA requires an independent inspector; Maharashtra a Returning Officer or SCEA-appointed presider (societies under 200 members). The host is not independent. | Owner | Add a distinct election-officer role the host cannot also hold; allow the society to name an external officer. |
| 8 | **Receipt PDF holding IP, mobile number, link ID** and the public "publish everything" promise. | Owner + privacy lawyer | Receipt only for the voter, omitting the choice (prevents vote-selling); IP kept in security logs for the one-year floor; public report pseudonymous/aggregate. |
| 9 | **Is a flat number personal data in a public report?** Treated as yes by inference (DPDP s.2(t)); s.3(c)(ii) public-data exemption does not cover self-published reports. | Privacy lawyer | Default report: totals, turnout, quorum, results; per-flat listing only as an opt-in with notice and consent. |
| 10 | **Photos and ID images.** Face images are sensitive in practice; Aadhaar numbers must not be stored or published (Aadhaar Act s.29(4), s.37, as read from the 2016 text). | Lawyer | Visual check only; record doc type, last 4 characters, checker, time, result. No ID images; provide a no-ID alternative path. |
| 11 | **Fiduciary vs processor.** Society = fiduciary, product = processor for rolls/votes; product is a fiduciary for its own billing, logs and defaults. | Lawyer | Data processing agreement with each society; separate notice for the product's own purposes. |
| 12 | **UP AOA: the 2010 Act text beyond s.14, Rules 2011, UP Ownership of Flats Act 1975, UP RERA interplay** not read; model bye-law 14 notice window of 2 to 7 days may be impractical. | Lawyer | Pull the Act from India Code (it returned 403) and the Rules from the UP gazette; allow societies to set longer notice. |
| 13 | **UP RWAs under the Societies Act**: no default quorum/notice/voting; rules sit in each society's bye-laws. | Owner | Onboarding step: society enters or uploads its bye-law values; engine refuses to start a meeting with unset required parameters. |
| 14 | **Maharashtra Apartment Ownership Act 1970 and MOFA 1963**: no usable source for association meeting rules; no pack created. | Lawyer | Out of scope until a lawyer confirms demand; support as a custom-bye-law society. |
| 15 | **US generic pack rests on Minnesota's 1993-based UCIOA text**, not the ULC 2021 act; RONR primary text not read. | Lawyer | Replace with official UCIOA text and RONR 12th before the generic US pack is trusted. |
| 16 | **US statutes were read via summaries and mirrors**; official sites refused. CA 2025/26 amendments (AB 2159, AB 2460), FL HB 1203 details, TX HB 886 not confirmed. | Lawyer | Spot-check every US figure against leginfo / flsenate / statutes.capitol.texas.gov. |
| 17 | **US: e-voting in CA excludes assessment votes** (5105) and requires member opt-in; FL requires a board resolution noticed 14 days; TX needs identity confirmation and receipt. | Owner | Encode as required preconditions in the US packs; engine blocks e-voting until they are met. |
| 18 | **Retention in India**: no retention period found in UP or Maharashtra primary text. | Lawyer | Make retention configurable per society; default to the longest bye-law or state-law duty; permanent for results. |
| 19 | **Whether DPDP Rules face a Supreme Court challenge or are amended**; any s.17(3) small-fiduciary exemption or s.16 restricted-country notification. | Lawyer | Re-check before 13 May 2027; host in India meanwhile. |
| 20 | **Aadhaar Act 2019 amendments, 2021 Regulations, masked Aadhaar and VID guidance, private verification limits for DL/EPIC/passport/PAN** not read. | Lawyer | Until verified, do not ask for Aadhaar at all; accept DL, EPIC, passport, or society roll check. |
| 21 | **Minutes**: UP has no deadline; MH bye-law 109 allows 3 months; CA 30 days. | Owner | Default internal target: publish draft minutes within 7 days, final after society approval; store the society's statutory deadline as a parameter. |
| 22 | **Payment via IT Services by Rajat Agarwal**: tax/GST and invoicing for a foreign-currency product, and receipt wording. | Owner / tax adviser | Out of scope for this study; flag for the commercial review. |
